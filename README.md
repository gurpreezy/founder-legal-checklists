# Founder Legal Checklists

Free, practitioner-grade checklists for startup founders raising venture capital, running a clean cap table, and preparing for an acquisition. Plain-English, vendor-neutral, and free to use, fork, and adapt.

These checklists distill what founders actually need to track at each stage — fundraising diligence, SAFE conversion, board governance, and M&A sell-side prep — without the jargon. Each one links to a deeper analytical explainer on **[blegal.ai](https://blegal.ai)**, a Silicon Valley startup and venture-finance knowledge base.

> **Not legal advice.** These are educational checklists, not legal, tax, or financial advice, and using them does not create an attorney–client relationship. Every financing and transaction turns on facts specific to your company. Verify anything here against your own documents and qualified counsel.
