# Cap Table Hygiene Checklist

Your cap table is the source of truth for who owns what. When it drifts from your signed documents, every financing slows down and every number becomes suspect. Keep it clean continuously — not the night before a raise.

> Educational checklist, not legal advice. Deeper reading at [blegal.ai — SAFE & Venture Capital](https://blegal.ai/knowledge/safe-venture-capital).

## Source of truth
- [ ] The cap table reconciles to **signed documents**, not just memory or a spreadsheet
- [ ] Every issuance maps to a board approval and a securities exemption
- [ ] Cap-table software (Carta, Pulley, etc.) matches the underlying paper — software is a record, not the authority

## Founders and common
- [ ] Every founder has a signed stock purchase agreement and vesting schedule
- [ ] **83(b) elections** filed within 30 days of each restricted-stock purchase (this deadline is unforgiving)
- [ ] Any founder departures handled with documented repurchase or vesting acceleration

## Options and the pool
- [ ] Option pool size and remaining availability are current
- [ ] Every grant has a board approval and a supporting **409A valuation**
- [ ] Promised-but-unissued options are tracked (they count as dilution)

## Convertibles
- [ ] All SAFEs, notes, and warrants logged with amounts, caps, discounts, and MFN/pro rata terms
- [ ] A model exists showing how they convert at the next priced round

## Before any financing or M&A
- [ ] Fully-diluted cap table reconciled end to end
- [ ] No orphaned IP assignments or unsigned equity docs
- [ ] A clean pro forma showing post-financing ownership
