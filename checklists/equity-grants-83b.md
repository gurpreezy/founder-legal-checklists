# Founder & Early-Employee Equity Checklist (incl. 83(b))

The cheapest equity mistakes to avoid are the early ones: unsigned IP assignments, missed 83(b) elections, and grants with no board approval. They're trivial to do right at the start and expensive to fix later.

> Educational checklist, not legal or tax advice. Deeper reading at [blegal.ai — Cap table hygiene context](https://blegal.ai/knowledge/safe-venture-capital) and the [term glossary](https://blegal.ai/knowledge/glossary).

## Founder restricted stock
- [ ] Each founder has a signed **stock purchase agreement** with a **vesting schedule** (commonly 4 years, 1-year cliff)
- [ ] **File the 83(b) election within 30 days** of the purchase — this deadline is unforgiving and cannot be cured late
- [ ] Keep proof of timely filing (certified mail receipt / confirmation)
- [ ] Confirm the company's repurchase right on unvested shares is documented

## IP assignment (do not skip)
- [ ] Every founder, employee, and contractor has signed a **present assignment** of IP to the company
- [ ] Confirm no IP was developed using a prior employer's resources or a university's
- [ ] This is the #1 issue flagged in financing and acquisition diligence

## Option grants
- [ ] An **equity incentive plan** is board- and stockholder-approved
- [ ] Every grant has its own **board approval** and a supporting **409A valuation** to set the strike price
- [ ] Track promised-but-unissued options — they still count as dilution
- [ ] Understand ISO vs NSO treatment for employees vs contractors

## Equity for non-US team members
- [ ] For international hires, confirm whether real options, RSUs, or **phantom stock** fits the local tax and exchange-control rules
