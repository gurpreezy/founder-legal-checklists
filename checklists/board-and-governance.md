# Startup Board & Governance Checklist

Control of the company is decided by two things most founders skim past: who sits on the board, and which decisions investors can veto. Get these right at each round and you keep the ability to run your company.

> Educational checklist, not legal advice. Deeper reading at [blegal.ai — Protecting board control at Series A](https://blegal.ai/knowledge/protecting-board-control-series-a) and the [term glossary](https://blegal.ai/knowledge/glossary).

## Board composition
- [ ] Count the seats after each round: founder seats, investor seats, independent seats
- [ ] Know who holds the **majority** of the board — and at what point that flips
- [ ] Independent directors should be genuinely independent, and mutually agreed
- [ ] Keep board size manageable; odd numbers avoid deadlock

## Protective provisions (investor veto rights)
- [ ] List exactly which actions require preferred approval: new financings, a sale, charter amendments, option pool increases, taking on debt
- [ ] Push to keep day-to-day operating decisions out of the veto list
- [ ] Watch for cumulative vetoes stacking across multiple investor classes

## Founder protections
- [ ] Understand your removal terms — what vote can remove a founder from the board or as an officer?
- [ ] Confirm founder vesting acceleration terms on a change of control (single vs double trigger)
- [ ] Keep clear, contemporaneous board minutes and consents for every major action

## Hygiene
- [ ] Approve every equity grant, financing, and material contract at the board level
- [ ] Maintain a clean minute book — diligence and disputes both start here
- [ ] Know your stockholders' inspection rights (e.g., Delaware Section 220)
