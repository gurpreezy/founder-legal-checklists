# Post-Money SAFE Conversion Checklist

Post-money SAFEs (the YC 2018+ standard) are simpler to sign than they are to convert. The dilution surprises happen at the priced round, not at signing. This is what to track so there are no surprises.

> Educational checklist, not legal advice. Full mechanics at [blegal.ai — Post-Money SAFEs: How They Work and Convert](https://blegal.ai/knowledge/post-money-safe-mechanics). Model your own numbers with the free [SAFE Dilution Calculator](https://blegal.ai/tools/safe-dilution-calculator).

## Before you sign each SAFE
- [ ] Record the **investment amount** and the **post-money valuation cap**
- [ ] Note whether it has a **discount**, an **MFN** ("most favored nation") clause, or a **pro rata** side letter
- [ ] Understand that each post-money SAFE locks the investor at `amount ÷ post-money cap` — and **founders absorb that dilution**, not the other SAFE holders

## While you're still on SAFEs
- [ ] Keep a running total of combined SAFE ownership: `Σ (amount ÷ cap)`
- [ ] Watch the total — stacked SAFEs dilute founders faster than most expect
- [ ] If any SAFE has an MFN, track whether a later, better SAFE retroactively improves it

## At the priced round
- [ ] Confirm whether the **cap or the round price** is the binding conversion term
- [ ] Model the new **option pool** — if it's created in the pre-money, it dilutes you and the SAFE holders, not the new investors
- [ ] Remember post-money SAFE holders are still diluted by the new money and the new pool — they are only protected from each other
- [ ] Reconcile the converted cap table against every signed SAFE before signing the Series A

## The mental model
A `$1,000,000` SAFE on a `$10,000,000` post-money cap = the investor locks **10%** of the company immediately before your priced round. Stack three of those and you have given away 30% before the Series A even starts.
